Avnit's Night Workout Meal Schedule Fat Loss & Muscle Fuel
Breakfast (8:00 AM)
High-Protein Metabolic Start
• 3 Whole Eggs (Scrambled or Boiled) with spinach/tomatoes.
• 1 slice Wholemeal toast or small bowl of oatmeal.
• Black coffee or green tea (no added sugar).
Lunch (1:00 PM)
Lean Protein & Local Complex Carbs
• 200g Grilled Chicken Breast or Local Fish (Mahi-Mahi/Tuna).
• 1 cup Steamed Dalo leaves (Rourou) or fresh green salad with lemon.
• 1/2 cup Boiled Cassava or Brown Rice.
Pre-Gym Fuel (5:30 PM - 6:00 PM)
Sustained Energy for 7:30 PM Gym
• 1 Banana or Apple.
• Handful of almonds or 1 scoop Whey Protein in water.
• Eaten 1.5 - 2 hours before your workout so your stomach is settled.
Post-Gym Dinner (8:45 PM - 9:15 PM)
Muscle Recovery & Overnight Fat Loss
• 220g Lean Chicken / Lamb curry (prepared with light oil) or Grilled Fish.
• Large serving of local vegetables (cabbage, eggplant, bhindi).
• Carb Control Rule: Keep starchy carbs (cassava/rice) very low or zero at this late dinner to maximize overnight fat oxidation while resting.
Hydration Target
4 Liters Daily
Drink water consistently throughout the day and keep a bottle on hand during your 7:30 PM session at Grace Road Gym.
