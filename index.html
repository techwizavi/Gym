Grace Road Gym • 5:30 AM Routine

🏋️‍♂️ Weight Goal: Progressive Strength
Day 1: Heavy Push & Chest 5:30 AM
1. Incline Treadmill Walk 5 Mins Warm-Up
Equipment: Treadmill
4.0 km/h at 4% incline to raise core body temp and prepare your chest/shoulder joints.
2. Barbell Bench Press 4 Sets × 8-10 Reps
Equipment: Flat Bench + Barbell
Lower bar controlled to mid-chest. Drive up firmly through your feet and chest.
▶ Watch @DeltaBolic Demo
3. Incline Dumbbell Press 3 Sets × 10-12 Reps
Target: 10 kg - 12.5 kg Dumbbells
Set bench at 30-45°. If 10 kg feels light, try 12.5 kg on your first 2 sets!
▶ Watch @DeltaBolic Demo
4. Standing Overhead Barbell Press 3 Sets × 8-10 Reps
Equipment: Barbell
Tighten core, press barbell straight overhead above head level without leaning backward.
▶ Watch @DeltaBolic Demo
5. Cable Triceps Pushdowns 3 Sets × 12-15 Reps
Equipment: Cable Machine
Keep elbows pinned to your ribs, extend fully downward to isolate triceps.
▶ Watch @DeltaBolic Demo
Day 2: Machine & Dumbbell Leg Power 5:30 AM
1. Leg Press Machine 4 Sets × 10-12 Reps
Target: 80 kg → Move to 85-90 kg
Feet shoulder-width on platform. Lower sled controlled to 90°. Drive forcefully through your mid-foot/heels.
▶ Watch @DeltaBolic Demo
2. Dumbbell Goblet Squats 3 Sets × 10 Reps
Equipment: Heavy Dumbbell
Hold dumbbell tight to chest. Squat low with chest up, forcing quads and core to work.
▶ Watch @DeltaBolic Demo
3. Dumbbell Romanian Deadlifts (RDL) 3 Sets × 10-12 Reps
Equipment: Dumbbells
Push hips straight back, sliding dumbbells down legs to feel a strong stretch in hamstrings and glutes.
▶ Watch @DeltaBolic Demo
4. Incline Treadmill Power Walk 15 Mins Finisher
Equipment: Treadmill
Set incline to 5-6% at 4.0 km/h. Smooth cardio burn to finish leg day.
Day 3: Heavy Pull & Back 5:30 AM
1. Barbell Bent-Over Rows 4 Sets × 8-10 Reps
Equipment: Barbell
Hinge at hips to 45°, pull bar straight to belly button level, squeezing upper back lats.
▶ Watch @DeltaBolic Demo
2. Cable Lat Pulldowns 3 Sets × 10-12 Reps
Equipment: Cable Pulldown Machine
Pull bar to upper chest while driving elbows down into side pockets.
▶ Watch @DeltaBolic Demo
3. Seated Cable Rows 3 Sets × 10-12 Reps
Equipment: Seated Cable Row Machine
Keep back tall, pull handle into abdomen squeezing shoulder blades tight together.
▶ Watch @DeltaBolic Demo
4. Barbell or Cable Bicep Curls 3 Sets × 12 Reps
Equipment: Barbell / Cable Machine
Keep elbows fixed at sides, curl weight up controlled to maximize bicep tension.
▶ Watch @DeltaBolic Demo
Day 4: Full Body Power & Barbell 5:30 AM
1. Barbell Deadlifts 4 Sets × 6-8 Reps
Target: 40-50 kg → Move to 55-60 kg
Chest up, flat back, drive through legs and extend hips. Highest calorie burning lift in the gym!
▶ Watch @DeltaBolic Demo
2. Standing Cable Woodchoppers 3 Sets × 12 Reps/Side
Equipment: Cable Machine
Rotate torso forcefully across body using core and obliques to trim waistline.
▶ Watch @DeltaBolic Demo
3. Dumbbell Farmer's Walk 3 Sets × 50 Meters
Equipment: Heavy Dumbbells
Pick up heavy dumbbells, stand tall, walk controlled. Excellent full-body grip and core builder.
▶ Watch @DeltaBolic Demo
Day 5: Treadmill HIIT & Core Burn 5:30 AM
1. Treadmill Interval Burn 20 Mins
Equipment: Treadmill
Alternate 1 min fast power walk / jog (6.5 km/h) with 1 min light walk (4.0 km/h) for 10 rounds.
2. Cable Core Crunches 3 Sets × 15 Reps
Equipment: Cable Machine (Rope attachment)
Kneel down holding rope at head level. Contract abdomen downward to flex abdominal wall.

3. Dumbbell Thrusters 3 Rounds × 45 Secs
Equipment: Dumbbells
Squat down and drive up overhead in one motion to burn massive calories.

Avnit's Early Morning Workout Meal Schedule Fat Loss & Energy Fuel
Pre-Workout Snack (5:00 AM)
Quick Digesting Morning Energy
• 1 Small Banana or 1 glass of water with a pinch of salt & lemon.
• Eaten 20-30 mins before 5:30 AM session so your body has quick fuel without a heavy stomach.
Post-Workout Breakfast (7:15 AM - 7:30 AM)
Muscle Recovery & Metabolism Engine
• 3 Whole Eggs (Scrambled or Boiled) with spinach/tomatoes.
• 1 slice Wholemeal toast or small bowl of oatmeal.
• Black coffee or green tea (no added sugar).
Lunch (12:30 PM - 1:00 PM)
Lean Protein & Refuel Carbs
• 200g Grilled Chicken Breast or Local Fish (Mahi-Mahi/Tuna).
• 1 cup Steamed Dalo leaves (Rourou) or fresh green salad with lemon.
• 1/2 cup Boiled Cassava or Brown Rice (ideal time for complex carbs to replenish glycogen after morning workout).
Afternoon Snack (4:30 PM)
Sustained Fuel
• Handful of almonds or 1 scoop Whey Protein in water (or 1 hard-boiled egg).
Dinner (7:30 PM)
Overnight Muscle Repair & Fat Oxidation
• 220g Lean Chicken / Lamb curry (prepared with light oil) or Grilled Fish.
• Large serving of local vegetables (cabbage, eggplant, bhindi).
• Carb Control Rule: Keep starchy carbs (cassava/rice/dalo) zero or near-zero at dinner to maximize overnight body fat burn while sleeping.
Hydration Target
4 Liters Daily
Drink water consistently throughout the day, starting right when you wake up at 5:00 AM.
