Avnit & Asna's 5-Day Plan

Grace Road Gym • Lovu, Lautoka

🏋️‍♂️ Avnit: 120 kg 🏋️‍♀️ Asna: 80 kg
Day 1: Chest & Push 60 Mins
1. Treadmill & Arm Circles 5 Mins
Equipment: Treadmill
Brisk 4.0 km/h warm-up walk combined with arm circles to lubricate shoulder joints.
2. Dumbbell Chest Press 3 Sets × 10-12 Reps
Equipment: Flat Bench + Dumbbells
Feet flat on floor for base stability. Lower weights with control keeping elbows at a 45° angle to protect shoulders. Press up directly over chest while exhaling.
▶ Watch @DeltaBolic Demo
3. Dumbbell Overhead Shoulder Press 3 Sets × 10-12 Reps
Equipment: Bench + Dumbbells
Sit tall with back supported. Press dumbbells straight overhead without arching your lower back. Lower back to ear height with full control.
▶ Watch @DeltaBolic Demo
4. Incline Dumbbell Bench Press 3 Sets × 10-12 Reps
Equipment: Incline Bench (30-45°) + Dumbbells
Set bench angle to 30-45 degrees. Press weights straight up over upper chest to build definition and shape up the upper torso.
▶ Watch @DeltaBolic Demo
5. Dumbbell Overhead Triceps Extension 3 Sets × 12 Reps
Equipment: 1 Dumbbell + Bench
Hold one dumbbell overhead with both hands forming a cup. Keep elbows pointing forward while lowering weight behind head, then extend up to squeeze triceps.
▶ Watch @DeltaBolic Demo
6. Forearm Plank 3 Sets × 30-45 Secs
Equipment: Exercise Mat
Rest on elbows and toes. Brace your abdomen tight, keeping hips in line with shoulders. Avoid letting lower back sag.
▶ Watch @DeltaBolic Demo
Day 2: Legs & Glutes 60 Mins
1. Bodyweight Warm-Up Squats 5 Mins
Equipment: Bodyweight
15 slow, controlled squats to warm up knee and hip joints before adding resistance.
2. Dumbbell Goblet Squats 3 Sets × 10-12 Reps
Equipment: 1 Dumbbell
Hold dumbbell vertically at chest height. Keep chest lifted high, push hips back and down until thighs are parallel to floor. Drive through heels to stand.
▶ Watch @DeltaBolic Demo
3. Romanian Deadlifts (RDLs) 3 Sets × 10 Reps
Equipment: Dumbbells
Hip hinge movement: keep back flat, knees soft, and push hips straight back while sliding dumbbells close down shins. Squeeze glutes at top.
▶ Watch @DeltaBolic Demo
4. Dumbbell Walking Lunges 3 Sets × 10 Steps/Leg
Equipment: Dumbbells / Bodyweight
Step forward, dropping back knee towards floor in a controlled 90° angle. Drive through front heel to step forward into next lunge.
▶ Watch @DeltaBolic Demo
5. Standing Calf Raises 3 Sets × 15 Reps
Equipment: Dumbbells / Step Platform
Hold weights at sides, push up onto balls of feet, hold stretch at top for 1 second, then lower down controlled.
▶ Watch @DeltaBolic Demo
6. Incline Treadmill Walk 10 Mins
Equipment: Treadmill
Set incline to 6-8% at 4.0-4.5 km/h. Do not hold handrails; engage core and glutes.
Day 3: Back & Pull 60 Mins
1. Light Rower / Treadmill Warm-Up 5 Mins
Equipment: Treadmill or Rower
5 minutes light cardio to prime back and arm musculature.
2. Dumbbell Bent-Over Rows 3 Sets × 10-12 Reps
Equipment: Dumbbells
Hinge at hips to 45 degrees with flat back. Pull dumbbells up towards hip pockets, squeezing lat muscles and shoulder blades together.
▶ Watch @DeltaBolic Demo
3. Lat Pulldown 3 Sets × 10-12 Reps
Equipment: Cable Machine / Barbell
Grasp bar wider than shoulders. Pull bar down to collarbone level while driving elbows down and back.
▶ Watch @DeltaBolic Demo
4. Single-Arm Dumbbell Row 3 Sets × 10 Reps/Side
Equipment: Flat Bench + Dumbbell
Support knee and hand on flat bench. Row weight straight up to waist level without twisting torso.
▶ Watch @DeltaBolic Demo
5. Dumbbell Bicep Curls 3 Sets × 12 Reps
Equipment: Dumbbells
Pin elbows against ribcage. Curl dumbbells smoothly up to shoulder level without swinging body. Lower under full control.
▶ Watch @DeltaBolic Demo
6. Hanging Knee Raises 3 Sets × 12 Reps
Equipment: Pull-Up Bar / Station
Hang from bar with core engaged. Raise knees up toward chest smoothly without swinging body momentum.
▶ Watch @DeltaBolic Demo
Day 4: Full Body Power 60 Mins
1. Dumbbell Deadlifts 3 Sets × 8-10 Reps
Equipment: Heavy Dumbbells
Stand shoulder-width apart. Squat down with flat back, grip dumbbells, drive through heels and extend hips to stand tall.
▶ Watch @DeltaBolic Demo
2. Dumbbell Floor Press 3 Sets × 10 Reps
Equipment: Dumbbells + Mat or Bench
Lie on floor/bench. Press dumbbells straight up over shoulders to build chest and triceps strength.
▶ Watch @DeltaBolic Demo
3. Dumbbell Farmer's Walk 3 Sets × 40 Meters
Equipment: Heavy Dumbbells
Hold heavy dumbbells at sides. Walk with proud tall posture and short controlled steps. Exceptional full-body conditioning exercise.
▶ Watch @DeltaBolic Demo
4. Russian Twists 3 Sets × 20 Reps
Equipment: Exercise Mat
Sit on mat, lean back slightly at 45°. Rotate torso side to side touching hands or weight to floor. Focus on abdominal rotation.
▶ Watch @DeltaBolic Demo
Day 5: HIIT & Core 60 Mins
1. Dumbbell Thrusters 3 Rounds × 45 Secs
Equipment: Light Dumbbells
Hold dumbbells at shoulders. Perform full squat, then drive up forcefully pressing dumbbells overhead in one fluid motion.
▶ Watch @DeltaBolic Demo
2. Kettlebell Swings 3 Rounds × 45 Secs
Equipment: Kettlebell or Dumbbell
Hip hinge movement! Drive hips forward forcefully to propel weight to chest height.
▶ Watch @DeltaBolic Demo
3. Mountain Climbers 3 Rounds × 45 Secs
Equipment: Mat
In strong plank position, drive knees to chest alternating rapidly while keeping hips low.
▶ Watch @DeltaBolic Demo
4. Cardio Interval Finisher 15 Mins
Equipment: Treadmill / Bike
Alternate 1 minute fast pace effort with 1 minute easy recovery.
Daily Nutrition Framework Fat Loss Focus
Breakfast (7:30 AM)
High-Protein Morning Start
• 3 Eggs (scrambled or boiled) or Egg white omelet with spinach & tomatoes.
• 1 slice Wholemeal toast or small serving of oats.
• Black coffee or green tea (no sugar).
Lunch (12:30 PM)
Lean Protein & Local Greens
• 180g-200g Grilled Chicken Breast or Fish (Local Mahi-Mahi / Tuna).
• 1 cup Steamed Dalo leaves (Rourou) or mixed green salad with lemon dressing.
• 1/2 cup Boiled Cassava or Brown Rice.
Pre-Gym Snack (4:30 PM)
Energy Booster
• 1 small banana or apple.
• Handful of almonds or 1 scoop Whey Protein in water.
Dinner (7:30 PM)
Recovery Meal
• 200g Lean Chicken / Lamb curry (light oil/coconut milk) or grilled fish.
• Plenty of local vegetables (cabbage, eggplant, okra).
• Keep starchy carbs low at dinner to maximize overnight fat burn.
