Avnit & Asna's 5-Day Plan

Grace Road Gym • Lovu, Lautoka

🏋️‍♂️ Avnit: 120 kg 🏋️‍♀️ Asna: 80 kg
Day 1: Chest & Push 60 Mins
1. Dumbbell Chest Press3 Sets × 10-12 Reps
Equipment: Flat Bench + Dumbbells
Feet flat on floor. Keep elbows at 45° angle while lowering weights.
2. Dumbbell Overhead Press3 Sets × 10-12 Reps
Equipment: Bench + Dumbbells
Sit tall, press weights overhead without arching lower back.
3. Forearm Plank3 Sets × 30-45 Secs
Equipment: Mat
Maintain a tight core and straight body line.
Day 2: Legs & Glutes 60 Mins
1. Dumbbell Goblet Squat3 Sets × 10-12 Reps
Equipment: 1 Dumbbell
Hold dumbbell at chest. Squat down low while keeping chest up.
2. Romanian Deadlifts (RDLs)3 Sets × 10 Reps
Equipment: Dumbbells
Push hips back, sliding dumbbells down legs to feel hamstring stretch.
Day 3: Back & Pull 60 Mins
1. Dumbbell Bent-Over Rows3 Sets × 10-12 Reps
Equipment: Dumbbells
Hinge forward 45°, pull dumbbells to hips, squeezing back muscles.
2. Dumbbell Bicep Curls3 Sets × 12 Reps
Equipment: Dumbbells
Keep elbows pinned to ribs. Curl up without swinging body.
Day 4: Full Body Power 60 Mins
1. Dumbbell Deadlifts3 Sets × 8-10 Reps
Equipment: Dumbbells
Squat down with flat back, drive through feet to stand straight.
Day 5: HIIT & Core 60 Mins
1. Kettlebell / Dumbbell Swings3 Rounds × 45 Secs
Equipment: Kettlebell or Dumbbell
Hip hinge movement! Drive hips forward to propel weight.
